AR Scrittura is an original monoline handwriting typeface designed by Alessandro Resch. Upright, separated letters and simple strokes give it the character of a restrained handwritten signature, with minimal flourishes and compact spacing.

Designed for product names, headings and short messages, the family currently includes one Regular style, Latin European characters, combining mark positioning and selected kerning pairs. It is not intended as a replacement for a highly legible interface or long-form reading typeface.

The project is released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, with editable UFO sources and an open-source build process.
