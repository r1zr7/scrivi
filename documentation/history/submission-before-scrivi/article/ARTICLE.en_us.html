Scrivi is an original monoline handwriting typeface designed by Alessandro Resch. Its upright, separated letters combine a light visual rhythm with compact spacing and restrained flourishes. The design is intended for names, headings and short personal messages.

The family offers one Regular style. Its character set includes Latin letters, accented forms, punctuation and selected symbols, with 392 encoded characters. OpenType features include selected kerning pairs, combining mark positioning and contextual dot removal for accented i and j. The font does not claim support for Greek, Cyrillic or Arabic.

Its simple strokes retain small variations in angle that reflect the handwritten design. Version 1.004 refines accent placement above tall lowercase letters, following earlier corrections to long s, kra and the separation of selected diacritics. These details were examined in large and small samples. The typeface is intended for expressive short text rather than as a universal interface or long-form reading font.

Scrivi is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The project includes editable UFO sources, original drawing data and a reproducible build process. Desktop TTF and locally hosted WOFF2 files are supplied in the direct distribution package.
